The video path

Encode only when the source needs it.

OpenHD keeps the camera path practical: use a suitable encoded stream directly, or encode raw frames with the best available hardware path.

ALREADY H.264 / H.265 / MJPEG

Use the stream

When a USB, IP or specialist camera already delivers a usable low-latency stream, OpenHD avoids a pointless decode-and-reencode cycle.

Bring your camera

From tiny CSI sensors to thermal payloads.

Support is shaped around real aircraft: low light, fixed focus, dual feeds, specialist sensors and cameras that encode for themselves.

Camera compatibility
CSI camerasRaspberry Pi, Arducam, VEYE and board-specific MIPI sensors
USB camerasRaw UVC and cameras with onboard H.264, H.265 or MJPEG encoding
HDMI sourcesAction and specialist cameras connected through supported HDMI-to-CSI bridges
Thermal + specialUSB thermal imagers, secondary feeds and custom unmanaged pipelines
