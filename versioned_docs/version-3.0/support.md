# Support

When asking for help, include the exact Albatross image name, Air and Ground models, radios, camera, and QOpenHD version.

1. Identify the failing path using [Troubleshooting](./general/troubleshooting.md).
2. Include what you expected, what happened, and the shortest steps to reproduce it.
3. Collect logs from both units and describe whether you tested the setup with one camera and matching builds.

For community support use [OpenHD Telegram](https://t.me/OpenHD_User) or [OpenHD Discord](https://discord.gg/P9kXs9N2RP).
